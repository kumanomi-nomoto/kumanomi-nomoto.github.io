## About This Blog

This blog is a collection of observations, analyses, experiments, and research notes related to cybersecurity.

The main topics include:

- Honeypot 
- IoT Malware / Botnets
- Vulnerability / Exploit Observation
- AI / Agent Security
- Network Security
- Malware Analysis

Based on real-world traffic, logs, and observations, I explore questions such as:

- What is actually happening?
- What might be the attacker's intent?
- How are different activities, infrastructure, and vulnerabilities connected?

## Purpose

The goal of this blog is not simply to introduce attacks or vulnerabilities.

Instead, I aim to examine observed data and better understand the relationships between attack activity, infrastructure, malware, and vulnerabilities.

This blog also serves as a personal record of my research, investigations, and learning.

## Disclaimer

The content published on this blog is intended for research and educational purposes only.

Some articles may include information related to IP addresses, domains, network traffic, vulnerabilities, or attack activity. However, the presence of such information does not necessarily indicate malicious activity by a particular individual or organization.

I do not intend to attribute malicious activity to any specific individual or organization without sufficient evidence.
